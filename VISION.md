# Phosphene — Vision & Creative Brief

> *The light you see when there's no light.*

---

## The Idea

A phosphene is the visual phenomenon of seeing light with no external source — the blooming colors behind closed eyes, the inner radiance that needs no sun. It's your own light, made visible.

Phosphene the app is built on that exact premise: take the happiness that lives inside you and give it a form you can see, navigate, revisit, and — if you choose — share. Not as a post. Not as a metric. As a living visual object that grows and breathes and connects.

This is not a journal, not a mood tracker, not a social feed. It's a new kind of thing.

---

## The Feeling It Must Produce

Land on Phosphene and feel **wonder** within 10 seconds. A quiet, slightly eerie, deeply beautiful kind of wonder — like stumbling onto something alive that wasn't meant to be seen. Then **curiosity**. Then the pull to add something of your own.

It should feel like:
- Discovering a bioluminescent bay at night
- Finding someone's constellation map in an old book
- Watching a murmuration from a hillside

It should never feel like:
- A productivity tool
- A social platform with metrics
- A prettier version of something that already exists

---

## The Canvas

An infinite, dark, zoomable canvas. Think of the Obsidian graph view as a distant ancestor — but where Obsidian is clinical and structural, Phosphene is organic, luminous, and alive. No hard grids. No rigid layouts. Things drift, breathe, pulse.

The visual language has three registers:

**Close (your constellation)**
Individual nodes fill the frame. You can see their shape, color, animation, and the text beneath them. Connections glow between them — some faint, some blazing. This is intimate. This is yours.

**Mid (your map)**
Pull back and see dozens of your nodes arranged in clusters — emotional weather patterns made visible. Color families drift together. Thread networks emerge. You can read the shape of a week or a year.

**Far (the collective cosmos)**
Keep zooming and your stars don't stop at a boundary. They thin out, and other lights appear — distant, dim, drifting. You're in the shared canvas now. The collective. Other people's quiet sparks, anonymous and beautiful, spread in every direction.

---

## Nodes

A node is a logged moment of happiness. It begins as a base form — a soft, luminous orb — and evolves.

**What shapes a node:**
- The words you wrote (processed for emotional tone, fed into AI similarity)
- The color you chose (influences glow color and cluster group)
- The shape you drew or selected (modifies the node's silhouette)
- The generated particle animation (a unique visual artifact, no two alike — conjured from your inputs like a fingerprint)
- Time (glow fades; connections accumulate; the node "ages" visually)
- Interactions (others' reactions subtly warm the glow)

**What a node carries:**
- The original entry (words, color, shape/drawing)
- Its particle animation artifact
- Its connection threads (AI-generated faint, manual bright, color resonance ambient)
- A timestamp and, if released to the collective, a location handle
- Any reactions or responses from others (collective nodes only)

**The glow mechanic:**
A node never disappears. But its glow fades with time unless revisited or touched. Recently logged nodes blaze. Old ones become quiet background light — still there, still part of the constellation, just resting. This creates a natural visual sense of *now* vs *then* without deleting anything.

---

## Connections

Three layers, always present, each visually distinct:

| Layer | Trigger | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| AI Semantic | Emotional/thematic similarity detected by embeddings | Faint, thin, barely-there threads |
| Color Resonance | Nodes share a color family | Soft ambient clustering, no explicit line |
| Manual | User deliberately links two nodes | Bright, solid, permanent thread with optional annotation |

The AI layer surprises you. It finds connections you didn't know existed — a memory of your dog in 2024 and a solo walk in 2026 share something the model recognized. The line appears quietly. You hover it and find out why.

---

## Logging a Moment

The entry flow must be **fast and beautiful**. A floating `+` that expands into a minimal panel. No long forms.

**Required:** Nothing. Everything is optional — even words. A color alone is a valid entry.

**Available inputs (mix and match):**
- A few words (1–3 sentences max encouraged, no hard limit)
- A color picker (sets the node's glow and cluster identity)
- A shape selector or freehand draw tool (modifies the node's silhouette)
- "Conjure" — triggers the generative particle animation for this entry

**Conjure:**
After any input, a single button generates a unique animated particle artifact from your inputs. Abstract, no two alike, feels almost alive. This is the reward for logging. The artifact becomes the node's face — what you and others see when zooming in.

---

## The Collective

The shared canvas is permanent and always growing. It is not a feed. There is no algorithm, no ranking, no engagement score. It's a cosmos.

**Identity:** Every contributor is an anonymous handle — auto-generated, poetic, location-tagged.
Examples: *"a wandering spark from Osaka"* / *"a quiet ember from Accra"* / *"a drifting light from São Paulo"*
Location is city or country level only, never precise.

**Interactions on collective nodes:**
- Hover to read
- Emoji react (reaction glows briefly then fades after 7 days — warmth passes through)
- Leave a brief response (visible, but also fades after 7 days)
- The node itself never fades from the collective canvas

**Releasing a private node:**
A deliberate, beautiful action. You choose a node from your constellation and "release" it — it drifts, visually, from your canvas into the collective. You can recall it if you change your mind.

---

## Navigation

A persistent ambient panel — minimal, dark, barely there — gives you two things:
1. **Where you are:** A soft indicator showing *Your Constellation* vs *The Cosmos*
2. **How to move:** A zoom control, and direct navigation options between the two worlds

The transition is never abrupt. Zooming out from your constellation into the collective is continuous — your stars don't vanish, they become part of a larger picture.

---

## Authentication

Optional. Always.

- **No account:** Experience the collective cosmos in full. Hover, read, react.
- **With account:** Add to the collective, build your private constellation, retain history.
- Anonymous sessions supported — you can log privately without signing up, data lives in local storage, with a prompt to save it by creating an account.

---

## Design Language

**Color:** Dark backgrounds always. Deep space black (#0a0a0f range). Node glows are the only real color — warm, saturated, never harsh.

**Typography:** One typeface. Clean, slightly geometric, excellent at small sizes. Minimal text everywhere.

**Motion:** Everything moves, but nothing rushes. Nodes drift slightly. Connections shimmer. The canvas breathes. Transitions are smooth and never instant.

**Sound:** Optional ambient layer. Off by default. If on: subtle generative audio that responds to canvas density.

**Empty states:** Beautiful, not sad. A new user's blank canvas should feel like an invitation, not a void.

---

## What Phosphene Is Not

- Not a social media platform. There are no followers, no likes as metrics, no algorithmic feed.
- Not a therapy tool. We don't analyze your happiness or give you scores.
- Not a clone of anything. The Obsidian graph is a structural ancestor, not a visual one. This should feel entirely new.
- Not a feature list. Every element serves the experience. If it doesn't, it doesn't ship.

---

## North Star

> Someone opens Phosphene at 11pm, adds a single color and two words about something small that made them smile, watches the particle animation conjure itself, and feels — for a moment — like their happiness has a shape.

That's the product. Everything else is in service of that moment.
